About — Rashank R

Designing with curiosity, empathy and intention.

I’m Rashank — a designer who believes the best work lives behind every click, swipe and drop-off, where there is a human being trying to accomplish something meaningful.

I bridge the gap between business objectives and human psychology. My approach starts with empathy and ends with intuition: by studying behavioural patterns and mental models, I craft digital experiences that feel second nature to the end user.

As a senior designer I lead with this perspective — mentoring design teams and collaborating with product leaders to create experiences that build trust, reduce effort and deliver value. That work has spanned SaaS and CRM platforms, healthcare and pharma, automotive, Web3 and DeFi, news and media, education, logistics, agriculture and travel — for clients across India, the GCC, EMEA/MENA, Japan and the United States.

I’m not a front-end developer, but I understand how front-end development works and have working knowledge of HTML and CSS concepts. That understanding shapes how I design within technical constraints and how closely I can collaborate with the engineers who build the work.

How I think

01

Understand before designing.

Confirm the problem is worth solving, and that it connects to a real business priority, before a single screen exists.

02

Design for people, not just screens.

Behavioural patterns and mental models come first; the interface is what falls out of understanding them.

03

Make complexity easier to understand.

Dense workflows, many states, heavy permissions — the job is to make all of it feel obvious in use.

04

Collaborate early with developers.

Feasibility review before commitment, pairing during build, and design QA on staging — not a handoff over a wall.

05

Design systems, not isolated screens.

Shared components and a real design-to-dev workflow — on one product this cut front-end effort by half.
