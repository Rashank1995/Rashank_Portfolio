01 — UX/UI Design · Automotive · Digital assets

BMW Dubai

BMW AGMC (Dubai, Sharjah & Northern Emirates) offers comprehensive vehicle leasing and financial services. As Dubai transforms into a global digital asset hub under VARA (Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority) frameworks, luxury automotive consumers increasingly demand digital asset payment capabilities for both Lease-to-Own and straight purchase models.

A four-and-a-half day offline process, rebuilt for eight minutes.

02

The challenge

Redesign the digital leasing journey on bmw-dubai.com/leasing-finance/ to introduce frictionless crypto payment integration (USDT, USDC, BTC, ETH), streamline high-value KYC/AML compliance, and drastically optimise lead-to-fulfilment UX metrics.

Three forces were working against the customer at once:

01

Converting crypto to fiat

Friction converting digital assets into local fiat (AED) in order to qualify for traditional bank auto-loans.

02

Offline verification

Lengthy in-branch verification processes stretching the journey across days rather than minutes.

03

Credit-bureau blind spots

Traditional bank financing rejections caused by a lack of local credit-bureau data — despite applicants holding substantial liquid digital assets.

03

The objective

Design a payment model flexible enough for how this audience actually holds value — allowing split payments (for example a down payment in USDT with monthly instalments by direct debit in AED), or a fully crypto-funded Lease-to-Own.

Cater to diverse tax and liquidity preferences rather than forcing a single funding route, and make compliance invisible through automated VARA-compliant exchange locking and instant KYC/AML sign-offs.
