Fujifilm Instax

An image-first mobile space where fans collect, style and celebrate the moments they care about — built on the nostalgic, tactile feel of instant film.

Client
Fujifilm
Role
UI/UX Designer
Platform
Mobile app
Year

Explore the full story (PDF) ↗

Making fandom tangible.

02

The challenge

Fans lacked a playful, dedicated digital space to meaningfully collect, style and celebrate their favourite moments and memories — leaving a gap for a more engaging, image-first experience.

03

The objective

Design a mobile experience that bridges this gap by bringing fan collections into a vibrant, image-first digital space, directly inspired by the nostalgic, tactile feel of instant film.

Process applied

This project followed my standard eight-phase practice, sized to the engagement. See the full process ↗

  • Scoping & Prioritization
  • User Research & Immersion
  • Synthesis & Problem Framing
  • Divergent Concepting
  • Interaction Design & Prototype
  • Validate — Testing
  • Build & Design QA
  • Measure & Iterate

05

Design system

The delivered system makes the brand’s energy usable rather than overwhelming:

01

Custom instant photo cards

The core content unit — a card that borrows the proportions and physicality of instant film.

02

Soft groupings

Used to create visual calm inside a high-energy brand, so dense collections stay readable.

03

Rounded navigation

A rounded navigation system with circular active states, echoing the product’s own form language.

04

Palette & rhythm

A lively visual identity in magenta, plum and cream, built on a precise 8 px grid rhythm.

07

Outcome

Delivered a fully realised design system and digital platform that makes fandom tangible — a consistent, image-first product language that scales from a single card to a full collection.
